About EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY

One ecosystem.
Many possibilities.

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY is a technology company providing digital solutions and online services. It operates EVOS Data Services, a platform for convenient data bundle purchases, while developing software, web platforms, AI tools, and innovative technology solutions.

EVOS Business Hub is the platform that brings it all together — the central ecosystem where every EVOXERA product lives. Under EVOS Business Hub today you'll find EVOS Data, our data bundle and airtime service, and EVOS GPT, our AI assistant — with more products joining the ecosystem as we build out business tools and a digital marketplace.

We started with what Ghanaians need most — fast, reliable data bundles and airtime — and we're building outward from there into AI, business automation, and a full digital marketplace.
