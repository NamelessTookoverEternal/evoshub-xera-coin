Our rules for integrations

Integrated is not owned

Integration does not transfer ownership or intellectual property rights to EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY.

Clear responsibility

Where EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY directly provides an integration or acts as a service provider, its responsibilities are disclosed. Otherwise the independent provider is responsible for its own product.

Payments disclosed first

Payment methods, charges, processing fees and refund conditions are shown before you confirm any payment.

XERA is optional

An integrated product may use fiat, free access, subscriptions or XERA where supported. Using XERA is never assumed.
