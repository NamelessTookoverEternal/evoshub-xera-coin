Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY is a technology business established in the Republic of Ghana. We operate EVOS Business Hub, XERA and the related websites and services available through https://evoshub.xyz.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Service and Corporate Policy.

2. Scope

This policy covers EVOS Business Hub and the products in the EVOXERA Ecosystem that link to it, including XERA.

Third-party products and services made available through the Integrated category are operated by independent providers. They may collect and use information under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

3. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name, username and email address.
  • Your login credentials, which we protect using appropriate security measures.
  • Your invite code and referral relationships, such as who invited you and who you have invited.

XERA activity

  • Your XERA balance, mining sessions, daily claims and transaction history.
  • Wallet addresses you connect or provide, and the transaction records linked to them.

Verification information

  • Where identity verification is required, for example before an eligible XERA transfer, the details and documents you submit for that purpose.

Payment information

  • When you pay through a payment provider such as Paystack, the provider handles your payment details. We receive transaction references, amounts and status. We do not intend to store full card numbers.

Messages and requests

  • Information you send us through contact forms, website requests, support channels or WhatsApp.

Technical information

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and log and security records.
  • Small items stored in your browser, such as your sign-in session and theme preference.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • Provide XERA mining, wallet, rewards, referral and related features.
  • Process purchases and payments for products and services.
  • Carry out identity verification where it is required.
  • Respond to your requests and provide support.
  • Protect the platform, detect abuse and fraud, and enforce our terms.
  • Maintain, measure and improve our services.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Why we may process it

We process personal information where you have given consent, where it is needed to provide the service you asked for, where we must comply with the law, or where it is necessary for our legitimate interests in running and securing the platform, provided those interests are not outweighed by your rights.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers that help us run the platform, such as hosting, database, authentication, messaging and payment providers. These currently include providers such as Supabase, Netlify, Fly.io, Render and Paystack.
  • Wallet and blockchain connection services, such as WalletConnect and TON Connect, when you choose to connect a wallet.
  • Integrated third-party providers, only where you choose to use their product, and as disclosed in that integration.
  • Authorities, regulators or courts where the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A successor, if the business or its assets are transferred, subject to this policy.

7. Blockchain records

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent by design. If you use a blockchain network, your wallet address and transaction details may be visible to anyone and cannot be altered or erased by us.

8. Where information is processed

Our providers may process information in countries outside Ghana. Where we use such providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the services, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal, accounting and regulatory requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where reasonably possible.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls and monitoring. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please keep your login details confidential and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

11. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Give you access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your information or close your account.
  • Stop or restrict certain processing, or withdraw a consent you previously gave.

To make a request, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity first. Some information must be kept to meet legal obligations, and blockchain records cannot be deleted.

12. Children

Our services are not intended for children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as your theme. You can clear this in your browser settings, but parts of the platform may then stop working.

14. Data protection law

We aim to handle personal information in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana and other applicable requirements.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services and legal obligations change. The revised version will show its effective date and will be available on the platform.
