How it works

How XERA mining works

STEP 1

Start mining

You start a mining session from your dashboard.

STEP 2

Session runs

The session runs for 24 hours, timed by the server — not your browser.

STEP 3

Return and claim

Once the session is eligible, come back and claim your reward.

STEP 4

Start again

After claiming, you can start a new 24-hour session right away.

The countdown shown in the app is for display only — the server is always the source of truth for when a session becomes claimable and how much it pays out. Daily claiming works the same way: a separate, simpler bonus on its own cooldown, independent of your mining session.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is XERA?

XERA is a digital ecosystem asset being developed by EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY as part of the broader EVOS ecosystem. It currently offers a wallet, 24-hour mining sessions, and daily claiming.

How does XERA mining work?

You start a session from your dashboard, it runs for 24 hours on the server's clock, and once it's eligible you return and claim the reward. Then you can start a new session.

How long does a mining session last?

24 hours from the moment you start it.

Do I need to keep the website open while mining?

No. Mining is tracked entirely on the server using its own timestamps — closing the tab or your browser doesn't stop or affect your session. The countdown you see in the app is just a display; it isn't what actually controls the timer.

Is my mining balance stored on the server?

Yes. Your wallet balance and every transaction are stored and validated server-side. Nothing about your balance is calculated or trusted from your browser.

Can I currently withdraw XERA?

No. Withdrawals are not active in the current version.

Can I currently transfer XERA?

No. Transfers between users are not active in the current version.

Can I currently buy or sell XERA?

No. Buying and selling are not active in the current version.

Is XERA currently listed on an exchange?

No. XERA is not listed on any exchange at this time.
