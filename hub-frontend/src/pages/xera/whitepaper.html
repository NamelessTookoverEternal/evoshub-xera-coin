What XERA is

3 · Mining

How XERA mining works

STEP 1

Start mining

Start a mining session from your dashboard.

STEP 2

Session runs

The session lasts 24 hours. It keeps running if you close the website or turn off your device.

STEP 3

Return and claim

When the session is complete, come back and claim your reward.

STEP 4

Start again

After claiming, you can start a new 24-hour session right away.

The platform tracks session time on its own servers, so the countdown on your screen is only a display. Daily claiming is a separate bonus with its own cooldown.

4 · Your wallet

Moving XERA to your wallet

Mined XERA is first recorded in your XERA account balance. Moving it to your own BNB Smart Chain wallet is an on-chain claim, and it is opening in stages. It is not available to everyone yet.

STEP 1

Verify your identity

Complete KYC verification. It is required before any on-chain claim.

STEP 2

Pay the one-time fee

A one-time processing fee applies, currently US$1, paid in USDT on BNB Smart Chain. The amount may change.

STEP 3

Claim on-chain

Choose how much to move. Your wallet will confirm the transaction. You pay the BNB network fee from your own wallet, so keep a little BNB in it.

Each on-chain claim is split by the contracts: 25% arrives in your wallet immediately, and 75% is locked in the vesting contract and releases gradually over about 180 days (roughly six months). You can see both parts on BscScan.

Moving XERA on-chain does not mean it can be sold. XERA is not listed on any exchange. See the disclosure.

7 · Trust

Security and transparency

  • Verified code. The token, vesting and mining distributor contracts are published and verified on BscScan, so anyone can read them.
  • Fixed supply. All 500,000,000 XERA were created once, when the token was deployed. The contract has no function to mint more.
  • Shared control. The token reserve and the administrative roles are held by multi-signature wallets (Safe) that need two approvals for each action.
  • Capped mining. The distributor can pay out at most its 75,000,000 XERA mining allocation, and each claim can be used only once.
  • Vesting. 75% of each on-chain claim is locked and releases gradually, which is enforced by the vesting contract.

Verified code is not a guarantee of value or of future listings. Read the disclosure.

9 · Questions

Frequently asked questions

Our FAQ page answers common questions about mining, KYC, the processing fee, vesting, scams and adding XERA to your wallet. These are the most common ones.

Is XERA listed on an exchange?

No. XERA is not listed on any exchange, and there is no official market price.

Can I withdraw my XERA to my own wallet?

On-chain claims are opening in stages, and they require KYC and a one-time processing fee. See Moving XERA to your wallet.

Do I need to keep the website open while mining?

No. Mining is tracked on our servers, so closing the tab or your browser does not affect your session.

How do I know I have the real XERA token?

Check that the contract address matches the one in The XERA token. Never trust a token by its name or logo alone.
