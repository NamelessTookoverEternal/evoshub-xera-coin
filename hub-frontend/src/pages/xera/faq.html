XERA

Frequently asked questions

Basics

What is XERA?

XERA is a digital asset built by EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY as part of the EVOS ecosystem. You earn it by mining in the XERA app. It is being introduced in stages.

What is the token called?

The token is named EVOXERA and its ticker is XERA. It runs on BNB Smart Chain and has a fixed supply of 500,000,000 XERA, which no one can increase.

Is XERA listed on an exchange?

No. XERA is not listed on any exchange, and there is no official market price. Mining XERA does not give it monetary value.

Can I buy, sell or transfer XERA?

Not through EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY at this time. Buying, selling and transfers between users are not active.

Mining

How does XERA mining work?

Start a session from your dashboard. After 24 hours, return to claim your reward and start another session.

How long does a mining session last?

24 hours from the moment you start it.

Do I need to keep the website open while mining?

No. Your session keeps running if you close the website or turn off your device. The countdown only shows when your session will be ready.

Where is my mining balance stored?

On the XERA platform. Your balance and transaction history are kept by the platform, and rewards are checked before they are credited. Mined XERA is not in your own wallet until you move it on-chain.

Moving XERA to your wallet

Can I withdraw my XERA to my own wallet?

On-chain claims are opening in stages, so they may not be available to you yet. When they are, you will see the option in your dashboard.

What do I need before I can claim on-chain?

You need to complete KYC verification and pay a one-time processing fee. The fee is currently US$1, paid in USDT on BNB Smart Chain, and it may change.

What happens when I claim?

25% of the claimed amount arrives in your wallet right away. The other 75% is locked in a vesting contract and releases gradually over about 180 days (roughly six months).

Do I need BNB in my wallet?

You pay the BNB network (gas) fee yourself, from your own wallet, so you need a small amount of BNB. The network fee is separate from the processing fee and goes to the network, not to us. Check the claim screen for the exact steps before you confirm anything in your wallet.

Safety and scams

Which contract address is the real XERA?

The official EVOXERA token contract on BNB Smart Chain is 0x7e1C8b087172d7E0DEe7d3Cb3b76624dB12b44D5. Anyone can create a token with the same name or ticker, so always check the address.

Will EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY ask for my seed phrase or private key?

Never. Do not share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone, including us. Be careful with messages that offer free XERA, ask you to connect your wallet to unknown sites, or promise guaranteed returns.

Where can I verify the contracts?

The token, vesting and mining distributor contracts are verified on BscScan. The full list of addresses is in the whitepaper.

Showing XERA in your wallet

How do I add XERA to my wallet?

In your wallet, choose to add a custom token on BNB Smart Chain and paste the official contract address above. The name, ticker XERA and 18 decimals should fill in automatically. Confirm the address matches before you add it.
