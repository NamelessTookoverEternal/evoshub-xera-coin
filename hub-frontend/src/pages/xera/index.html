How XERA mining works

  • A mining session lasts 24 hours from the moment you start it.
  • You need to come back to claim your reward and start another session — it doesn't renew on its own.
  • Every session and every reward is validated by the server, not the app on your phone.
  • XERA cannot be withdrawn or transferred in V1 — that's coming in a later phase.
  • The displayed reward is an estimate and is subject to the platform's mining rules, which can adjust the rate as the network grows.
