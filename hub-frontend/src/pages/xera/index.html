Verify Your Identity

Identity verification is required before your first eligible XERA transfer. You can continue mining while verification is pending, but verification must be completed before transferring.

You will be taken to our verification partner to photograph your ID or passport and take a selfie. We never receive or store those images. After the automatic check, our team confirms the result.

Reviewed manually by our team. We keep only your name, country, ID type and the last 4 characters of your ID number. Do not upload or send photos of documents. Never share a seed phrase or wallet password.

First-Time Transfer Processing Fee

  • A one-time processing fee is required before your first eligible XERA transfer.
  • It is not the price of XERA and it is not blockchain gas. Network fees are separate.
  • Payment does not guarantee a successful transfer. Wallet verification and all other requirements still apply.

    How XERA mining works

    • A mining session lasts 24 hours from the moment you start it.
    • You need to come back to claim your reward and start another session — it doesn't renew on its own.
    • Every session and every reward is validated by the server, not the app on your phone.
    • XERA cannot be withdrawn or transferred in V1 — that's coming in a later phase.
    • The displayed reward is an estimate and is subject to the platform's mining rules, which can adjust the rate as the network grows.
