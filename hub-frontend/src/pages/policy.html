1. Introduction

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY is a technology development and digital innovation business established to create, manage, develop and integrate technology-driven products and services.

Our operations are organised around the development of an interconnected digital ecosystem, with EVOS Business Hub serving as a central access point for our products, services and approved integrations.

This policy establishes the principles, standards and responsibilities guiding our operations.

2. Ownership and business structure

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY is the operating business responsible for its independently developed and owned products.

Its business activities are organised into two principal categories:

A. ECOSYSTEM

The Ecosystem category contains products and services developed, owned or directly operated by EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY.

These may include:

  • XERA and its associated ecosystem infrastructure.
  • EVOS Data Services.
  • EVOSGPT.
  • Evolution 1: The Runner.
  • Other current and future EVOXERA products.

Ownership and intellectual property rights remain subject to applicable agreements and product-specific arrangements.

B. INTEGRATED

The Integrated category contains third-party products, services, applications and technology solutions made accessible through EVOS Business Hub or connected to the EVOXERA ecosystem.

These may include:

  • Independent business applications.
  • Partner-developed digital products.
  • Third-party technology services.
  • External payment and service providers.
  • Approved XERA-enabled or fiat-enabled integrations.

Integration does not automatically transfer ownership or intellectual property rights to EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY.

3. Innovation policy

We encourage continuous innovation, research, experimentation and responsible development.

All products should aim to provide meaningful functionality, solve identifiable problems and contribute positively to the digital environment.

4. Technology and security policy

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY seeks to:

  • Maintain reasonable technical and organisational security safeguards.
  • Protect platform accounts and authorised access.
  • Monitor and address vulnerabilities.
  • Improve system reliability and availability.
  • Prevent unauthorised access, misuse and fraudulent activity.
  • Maintain appropriate operational and technical records.

No digital platform can guarantee uninterrupted or completely risk-free operation.

5. Privacy and data protection policy

We respect the privacy of users, customers, developers and business partners.

Personal information must be collected and processed for legitimate, disclosed purposes and handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

We aim to:

  • Collect only information necessary for stated purposes.
  • Explain how information is used.
  • Apply reasonable security measures.
  • Restrict unauthorised access.
  • Respect applicable rights relating to personal information.
  • Manage information retention and deletion responsibly.

Detailed practices must be explained in the applicable Privacy Policy.

6. Digital asset and XERA policy

XERA is intended to function as a digital utility within its designated ecosystem.

Its availability, functionality, distribution, transferability and supported networks are governed by its specific documentation and technical arrangements.

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY does not promise investment returns, guaranteed appreciation, guaranteed liquidity or future market value.

Users must distinguish between testnet activities, simulated balances and actual mainnet digital assets.

7. Financial and payment policy

Products may support different payment arrangements, including:

  • Fiat currency.
  • Approved electronic payment services.
  • Digital asset payments where supported.
  • Other authorised payment integrations.

Payment methods, applicable charges, processing fees and refund conditions must be disclosed before transactions are completed.

An integration with a payment provider does not mean that EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY operates that provider.

8. Partnership policy

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY may collaborate with independent developers, businesses, service providers and institutions.

Partnerships must have clearly defined responsibilities, ownership arrangements, service expectations and applicable commercial terms.

9. Intellectual property policy

Company-owned software, designs, branding, documentation, original content and technology are protected by applicable intellectual property laws.

Users and partners may not reproduce, redistribute, modify or commercially exploit protected materials without appropriate authorisation.

Third-party intellectual property remains the property of its respective owners.

10. Ethical conduct

We expect responsible conduct from users, employees, contractors, developers and partners.

We do not support:

  • Fraudulent activities.
  • Unauthorised access to systems.
  • Deceptive business practices.
  • Intellectual property infringement.
  • Misuse of personal information.
  • Malicious activities affecting our platforms or users.

11. Policy amendments

EVOXERA TECHNOLOGY may revise this policy as its products, services, partnerships and legal obligations evolve.

Material changes will be communicated through appropriate company channels.
